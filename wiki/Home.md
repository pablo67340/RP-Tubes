# RP Tubes Wiki

**RP Tubes** revives the RedPower 2 (Tekkit Classic, pr5b2) pneumatic tube transport
system for **Minecraft 1.19.2 / Forge 43+** — original behaviors, textures, GUIs, and
routing logic, plus the two Automatic Crafting Tables from BuildCraft and Tubestuff.

## Pages

- **[Soft Dependencies](Soft-Dependencies)** — what RP Tubes integrates with when
  present (JEI, ProjectRed, ProjectE, BuildCraft CE, other tin mods) and what it
  needs (nothing).
- **[Modder API](Modder-API)** — Java API for mod developers: connecting your
  inventories to tubes, injecting items into the network, and implementing your own
  tube-connectable blocks.
- **[Datapack Recipes & Tags](Datapack-Recipes-and-Tags)** — the
  `rptubes:alloy_smelting` recipe JSON format and every tag RP Tubes reads or
  provides, for pack makers.
